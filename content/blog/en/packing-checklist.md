Half of pre-trip stress is the fear of forgetting something. Save this list and go through it the day before you fly.

## Documents

- Passport (valid for at least 6 months after your return)
- E-tickets and hotel voucher — on your phone and printed just in case
- Insurance policy and assistance phone number
- Cash in dollars or euros: Russian bank cards do not work abroad in most countries. For domestic trips — a card plus a spare

## First-aid kit

- Painkillers and fever reducers
- Something for an upset stomach
- Plasters and antiseptic
- SPF 50 sunscreen — it costs twice as much at the resort

## Tech

- Chargers and a power bank (carry-on only!)
- Universal plug adapter
- Headphones for the flight

## For the beach

Swimwear, reef shoes for Egypt and Thailand, a snorkelling mask, light long-sleeved clothing for sun protection.

## For the mountains

Thermal underwear, fleece, gloves, balaclava, goggles and helmet. Skis are easier to rent on site — no need to pay to fly them.

## Tip

Take photos of your passport and insurance and save them to the cloud. If documents get lost, they'll be much easier to replace.
