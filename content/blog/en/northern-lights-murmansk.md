The northern lights are the main reason travellers head to the Kola Peninsula. No international passport is needed, and the flight from Moscow takes about two and a half hours.

## When to go

The season runs from September to the end of March. **December–January** is polar night: dark nearly all day, but cloudier. **February–March** still has dark nights and clearer skies more often.

## Where to watch

- **Teriberka** — the Barents Sea coast with minimal light pollution
- **Around Murmansk** — hills 20–40 minutes from the city, good for short trips
- **The Khibiny and Kirovsk** — easy to combine with skiing

## How to forecast

Watch the cloud forecast and the Kp index. At Murmansk's latitude **Kp 2–3** is often enough.

## How to shoot on a phone

1. Use night mode or manual mode with a 3–10 second exposure.
2. Put the phone on a tripod or a steady surface.
3. Turn off the flash and dim the screen.

## What to wear

Thermal underwear, a wool sweater, a down jacket, warm boots and mittens. Thermal suits are provided on our Murmansk tours.
